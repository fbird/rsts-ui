Training topic: IADC WellSharp Well Servicing Coiled Tubing Courses
Course Desciption: 
The IADC WellSharp Well Servicing Coiled Tubing course is a specialized training program for individuals involved in coiled tubing operations, focusing on well control principles and risk management. It is designed for service company equipment operators primarily responsible for managing well control during coiled tubing operations. The course, which is part of the IADC WellSharp program, covers pressure control equipment, barrier philosophy, operational considerations, and emergency procedures specific to coiled tubing.
