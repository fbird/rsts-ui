Date: 2025/04/05 - 2025/07/15
Duration： 10 days
Training topic: Drilling Equpiment, Techniques and Procedures On Site Training
Course Desciption: 
This course bridges theoretical knowledge with real-world application by focusing on on-site, interactive training sessions, including live demonstrations, equipment handling, and procedural simulations. Participants will gain a deep understanding of: The structure, function, and maintenance of drilling equipment, Step-by-step drilling procedures from spudding to completion, Safety protocols and environmental considerations, Troubleshooting and problem-solving during active drilling operations, This training is ideal for both entry-level and experienced personnel seeking to improve their technical competencies or refresh their skills in line with the latest industry standards and technologies.
