## Why

The current homepage presents RSTS primarily as an academy and repeats similar delivery messages,
which understates the company's broader role across accredited training, technical competence, HSE,
and rig assurance. The homepage should establish a clear corporate position, communicate the most
credible differentiators from the company profile, and guide both individual candidates and
corporate buyers toward the appropriate next action.

## What Changes

- Reposition the homepage around RSTS as an oil and gas competence and operational assurance
  partner, rather than only a course provider.
- Refine the hero message so the RSTS brand, accredited training, and rig assurance scope are clear
  within the first viewport.
- Organize the homepage into a deliberate narrative: brand position, recognition, integrated
  capabilities, accredited role pathways, operational evidence, rig assurance, flexible delivery,
  and conversion.
- Present four primary capability pillars: Accredited Well Control, Technical Training, HSE and
  Safety, and Rig Assurance.
- Introduce role-based IWCF and IADC pathway content for foundation personnel, drillers, and
  supervisors instead of relying on a flat catalogue of course names.
- Give DROPS surveys, rig inspection, technical audit, and commissioning a distinct homepage
  presence so rig assurance is not treated as a secondary training item.
- Consolidate repeated centre-based, portable simulator, corporate programme, and client-site
  delivery content into one flexible-delivery section.
- Use recent programme imagery and approved project evidence to demonstrate operating context and
  practical delivery.
- Retain certificate verification as a trust and post-training assurance feature.
- Add a clear closing conversion path for training enquiries, corporate programme planning, and rig
  assurance requirements.
- Require public claims, accreditation identifiers, centre locations, instructor counts, delivery
  lead times, and client references to be verified before publication.
- Avoid reproducing long mission statements, exhaustive course lists, or unverified superlatives on
  the homepage; route detailed information to the relevant catalogue and company pages.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `corporate-homepage`: Defines the RSTS homepage narrative, required content sections, evidence and
  factual-claim constraints, responsive presentation, and conversion paths for candidate and
  corporate audiences.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

- Primary page: `src/views/home/HomeView.vue`.
- Homepage copy, content data, section hierarchy, responsive layouts, and supporting visual assets
  will be affected.
- Existing course catalogue, certificate verification, company profile, and contact routes will be
  reused where appropriate; no API contract changes are expected.
- Marketing or operations stakeholders must confirm current accreditation status and identifiers,
  ISO certifications, training-centre locations, instructor count, worldwide delivery commitments,
  and permission to publish client names or logos.
